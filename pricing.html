Questions

What happens after the 14-day trial?You'll be asked to subscribe. Until you do, your inbox still shows matches but you can't push new ones into Pipedrive. Nothing you've already pushed is touched.

Is there a contract or minimum term?No. Monthly plans can be cancelled any time; annual plans run for the year you paid for.

Who handles payment?Our payments and invoicing are handled by Paddle, who act as merchant of record. VAT is calculated and included on your invoice automatically.

Does it change our Pipedrive setup?No. TenderInbox never creates custom fields or changes your pipelines. It adds Leads or Deals only when you click, plus an organisation, a note and a deadline activity on each.

Which Pipedrive plans does it work with?All of them, including Essential.

How do I cancel?From the billing link in TenderInbox Settings, or uninstall the app from Pipedrive. We delete your access token immediately and your other data within 30 days.

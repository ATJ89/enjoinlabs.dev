UK public-sector tenders, inside Pipedrive

BidRadar watches the UK government's Find a Tender service for notices that match your business, shows you the ones worth a look, and pushes the ones you choose into your Pipedrive pipeline — as a Lead or a Deal, your call.

How it works

  • Tell us what you sell: keywords, CPV categories, regions and contract values.
  • We check Find a Tender every day and keep a list of matching notices.
  • You review the list inside Pipedrive and push only the ones you want to pursue.
  • Deadlines, buyer and notice link land on the record. Nothing is created without you clicking.
BidRadar is in private beta. If you use Pipedrive and bid for UK public-sector work, get in touch and we'll set you up.

UK public-sector tenders, inside Pipedrive

TenderInbox is a tender-alert and procurement-intelligence service. It monitors the UK government's Find a Tender and Contracts Finder services for published notices that match your business and shows the matching ones inside your Pipedrive account, where you can add the ones you want to your pipeline — as a Lead or a Deal, your call. TenderInbox does not send messages to anyone, and does not collect or sell contact data; it reads public notices and shows them to you.

How it works

  • Tell us what you sell: keywords, CPV categories, regions and contract values.
  • We check Find a Tender and Contracts Finder every day and keep a list of matching notices.
  • You review the list inside Pipedrive and add only the ones you want to pursue to your pipeline.
  • Deadlines, buyer and notice link land on the record. Nothing is created without you clicking.
TenderInbox is in private beta. If you use Pipedrive and bid for UK public-sector work, get in touch and we'll set you up.
